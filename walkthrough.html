Asha Rao, here's where KiranaLoop stands
Pilot Ready
You have early clarity and can run a controlled pilot.
⬇ Download PDF
Your snapshot
KiranaLoop is a strong example of building proof before product — a manual WhatsApp MVP over an overbuilt billing app, targeting the highest-value pain (reorders).
What's working
High-leverage concierge MVP that tests the core value manually before writing code.
Excellent stakeholder clarity — distributor as trusted influencer, elder as potential blocker.
Worth testing
Whether customers consistently convert from WhatsApp nudges into repeat orders over time.
Whether manual fulfilment creates too much overhead for the shopkeeper.
A−100%A+
7 · Results — stage-forward, AI-written
Your 7-day plan
Day 1Define pilot success metrics with the 2 shops.
Day 2Draft local-language WhatsApp reminder scripts.
Day 3Launch the manual concierge service.
Day 4Track responses, forward orders, verify deliveries.
Day 5Interview both shopkeepers on comfort + reactions.
Day 6Share early metrics with the distributor for 3 intros.
Day 7Calc reminder→order conversion + time per store.
Your pitch, sharpened
We help Tier-2 kirana stores run automated WhatsApp reorder campaigns to win back the 15–20% of repeat sales they lose to quick-commerce apps.
Something to sit with
If your nudges double a store's delivery orders, how will the shopkeeper fulfil them without getting overwhelmed?
